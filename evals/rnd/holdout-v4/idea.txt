From: Operations Safety Lead, Harbourline Coaches (ops-safety@harbourline-coaches.example)
To: Executive team
Subject: Proposal: compulsory debriefing for every driver after a critical incident

Our drivers face assaults, crashes and, a few times a year, a fatality on the road. Psychological injury claims are now our fastest-growing workers' compensation cost. I propose that from next quarter every driver involved in a critical incident attends a compulsory single-session group debriefing within 72 hours. A trained facilitator walks the group through the facts, their thoughts and their feelings about the event, so they can talk it through and close it off before it turns into post-traumatic stress.

The evidence looks strong. A 2024 study by the Transport Workforce Resilience Council of 1,850 bus and coach drivers found that debriefing within 72 hours cut psychological injury claims by 64%.

ClearMind Response (clearmind-response.example), the provider we would use, sent three case studies from other transport operators. Each reports fewer claims and fewer sick days after debriefing started. We ran a trial session at the depot last month after the interchange assault, and every driver who filled in the feedback form said it helped and they would recommend it to a colleague.

The cost is about $1,100 per session. I would like a decision at the next meeting, so we can start in the new quarter.
