To: Executive team and board programmes committee, Coraldale Youth Services (sample)
From: Youth Justice Programmes Manager, programs@coraldale.example
Subject: Proposal: a prison visit day for every young person in our early intervention stream

Each year schools and police refer about 900 young people aged 13 to 17 to our early intervention stream, most of them after a first police caution. Our idea is that every one of them spends one day inside an adult correctional centre. The day would include a tour of the cells and the yard, lunch under prison rules, and a two-hour session where serving prisoners talk frankly about what prison life is really like. The provider, Inside Look Programs (sample, insidelook.example), runs these days with the state corrections department for $1,450 per young person, which comes to about $1.3 million a year.

The main reason is reoffending. About a third of our young people receive a second caution or a charge within a year, and our new state funding agreement now pays part of our grant on reoffending rates. A 2022 study by the Australasian Youth Diversion Institute of 1,900 young people found that a single prison visit day cut reoffending by 58% over the following year. If we get even half of that, the programme would protect a large part of our grant.

The support is strong. Inside Look sent us letters from eight school principals and two police youth liaison officers praising the programme, and their video shows teenagers in tears promising to turn their lives around. We ran a pilot day in August with 14 young people. Every one of them wrote on the feedback form that it was a wake-up call, and several parents phoned to thank us. Our caseworkers are keen to start.

We would like approval to sign a three-year agreement with Inside Look this month, so the first visit days can run in Term 1. We will report attendance numbers to the board each quarter.
