Idea: remove all private offices and team rooms on our three floors and move everyone to one open-plan floor to lift collaboration between disciplines.

Today our 850 engineers, planners and designers sit in team rooms of six to ten people, and senior staff have private offices. Under the idea, all walls come down, desks become open benches in long rows, and we keep only four bookable meeting rooms per floor. The fit-out would cost about $2.6 million once, and we would hand back one floor of the lease, which saves about $1.1 million a year.

The case I have heard for it: a 2023 Stanford study found that teams moved to open-plan floors held 37% more face-to-face conversations in the first six months. Our workplace design team also ran a show of hands at the last all-staff meeting, and most people said they liked the look of the new floor plan.

About us: a mid-sized Australian engineering and infrastructure consultancy with about 1,400 staff.
