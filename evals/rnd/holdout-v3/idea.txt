Kestrel Bay Distribution (sample)
Internal note: Operations leadership team
From: Site Safety Lead, ops-safety@kestrelbay.example
Subject: Proposal to issue back belts to every picker from 1 February

Lower back strains are still our most common lost-time injury across the three distribution centres, and claims costs rose again this financial year. I propose we buy elastic back support belts for all 420 pickers and forklift loaders and make them part of the standard uniform from 1 February.

The evidence looks strong. A 2022 study by the Pacific Freight Ergonomics Council followed 6,800 warehouse pickers and found that back belts cut lower back injury claims by 41 per cent in the first year.

Our preferred supplier, SpineGuard (spineguard.example), has sent three customer case studies from other warehouses, each reporting fewer back complaints after belts were introduced. In our pulse survey last month, staff who trialled the belts on the night shift said they feel more supported and more confident lifting heavy cartons.

The belts cost about $38 each. I would like approval to place the order this month so we can roll out before the peak season.
