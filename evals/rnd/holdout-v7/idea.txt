The evidence looks strong. The vendor's brochure says the glasses also help people fall asleep faster after an evening shift, which should bring down sick days.

We ran a two-week pilot with 22 consultants on the night shift team. Nineteen of them said on the feedback form that their eyes felt less tired, and several said they slept better. The team leaders are keen, and two of them have already bought their own pairs.
