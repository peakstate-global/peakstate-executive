To: Executive team, Harbourline Fresh Markets (sample)
From: People and Culture, harbourline.example
Subject: Proposal: a company-wide staff wellness programme from 1 February

Our idea is to roll out a full wellness programme to all 4,200 store and distribution centre staff. It would start with a free on-site health check (blood pressure, cholesterol, weight) and an online health risk survey. Staff would then get a twelve-week step challenge, nutrition and stress modules from a dietitian, and a gift card of up to $150 for taking part. The vendor, Vitalpath Workplace (sample, vitalpath.example), would run it for $310,000 a year.

The main reason is unplanned sick leave, which cost us about $2.9 million last year, and staff turnover in our stores. A 2023 study by the Australasian Retail Wellbeing Council of 2,600 store staff found that programmes like this cut unplanned sick leave by 41% within the first year. If we get even half of that, the programme pays for itself more than twice over.

The support is strong. Vitalpath gave us six case studies from retail and logistics clients, each showing lower absence and better morale after their programme. We ran a two-week taster at our Northgate store in September, and the feedback forms were very positive: staff said they felt more energised, and almost everyone said they would recommend it to a colleague. Several store managers have asked when it will start.

We would like approval to sign a three-year contract with Vitalpath this month, so the health checks can begin in February. We will report participation numbers to the executive team each quarter.
